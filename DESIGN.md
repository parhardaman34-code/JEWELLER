## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal grandeur of royal Indian heirloom craft, translated for a discerning modern clientele. The aesthetic combines timeless architectural balance with tactile luxury, celebrating ancestral goldsmithing traditions, bespoke bridal trousseaus, and authentic BIS Hallmarked purity.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Regal & Authoritative:** Evoking dynasty-grade heritage, palace architecture, and enduring family legacies without feeling dated or inaccessible.
- **Warm Hospitality:** Infused with classic Punjabi warmth, celebratory abundance, and intimate personal consultation.
- **Meticulous Precision:** Conveying unmatched gemstone certification, metallurgical purity, and master artisan finesse.

### Visual Style Movement
The design movement is **Heritage Editorial Luxury**. It pairs deliberate, generous negative space with opulent, jewelry-box layering:
- Warm ivory and deep obsidian surfaces create museum-grade contrast.
- Polished metallic gold borders, hairline framing, and subtle velvet-like depths elevate product imagery to artifact status.
- Restrained, symmetrical micro-details recall traditional Indian *jali* (lattice) work and architectural arch silhouettes while remaining strictly functional and modern.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in authentic metals and jewel tones, anchored against warm, organic neutrals rather than stark synthetic whites or pure blacks.

### Roles & Accents
- **Primary (`#B8860B` - Antique 22K Gold):** Used for focal brand accents, active states, key interactive badges, and certified hallmark highlights. Secondary tints include `#D4AF37` for metallic gradients and luminous rim highlights.
- **Secondary (`#1B4D3E` - Imperial Emerald):** Reserved for institutional trust seals, BIS Hallmark indicators, bespoke appointment actions, and high-value verification accents.
- **Tertiary (`#721829` - Royal Ruby / Deep Burgundy):** Serves as an emotive accent for bridal collections, ceremonial callouts, special commissions, and festive alerts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` to `#1A1715`):** 
  - Canvas base rests on warm cream and polished parchment (`#FAF7F2` for main light backgrounds, `#F3EDE2` for card surfaces).
  - Deep obsidian charcoal (`#1A1715` and `#12100E`) forms high-contrast hero modules, footer realms, and royal showcase panels.
  - Text typography utilizes `#241E1A` to avoid digital harshness while retaining AAA contrast against ivory backdrops.

## Typography

The typography hierarchy sets up a dramatic dialogue between regal heritage and pristine contemporary legibility:

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in **Playfair Display**. Features transitional contrast, sharp serifs, and classical proportions. Used for major collection intros, ceremonial milestones, and heirloom storytelling.
- **Body & Numerical Specifications:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Provides high legibility for delicate technical gold purities (e.g., 22KT / 916 BIS), gram weights, certification codes, and pricing breakdown tables.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with elevated letter spacing (0.08em to 0.12em) and small-caps styling where appropriate to evoke bespoke boutique engraving.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around an intentional **12-column editorial fluid grid**, anchored within a maximum canvas width of 1440px to ensure luxury compositions do not stretch unnaturally on ultra-wide screens.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Bespoke Breathing Room:** Sections use generous vertical breathing spaces (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem–6rem between thematic showcase clusters), allowing single ornaments, bridal suites, and choker sets to command individual attention.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Feature spotlights utilize deliberate multi-column offsets (e.g., an 8-column high-resolution hero paired with a 4-column contextual provenance narrative).

### Responsive Adaptation
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 4rem outer canvas margins. Full side-by-side bridal gallery pairing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, 2rem margins. Product matrices shift into 2-column rhythms with sticky header anchors.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1.25rem margins. Stacked layout with edge-to-edge swipeable jewelry carousels that reveal peeking card margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use aggressive digital drop shadows. Instead, it relies on **ambient jewel-box depth and subtle metallic outlines**.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Surface:** Warm Cream (`#FAF7F2`) or Midnight Obsidian (`#1A1715`). Flat, non-reflective base foundation.
2. **Pedestal Tier (Cards & Vitrines):** Lifted via an ultra-subtle warm shadow blended with gold and amber tones: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(184, 134, 11, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.04)`. Outlined with a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(184, 134, 11, 0.22)`).
3. **Elevated Overlays & Modals:** Used for Bespoke Consultation drawers and Karat calculators. Floating state uses a deep velvet shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(18, 16, 14, 0.35)`, backed by a gentle warm frosted blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.88)` on light or `rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.88)` on dark).
4. **Interactive Focus:** Buttons and cards do not lift sharply on hover; they intensify their inner golden halo and crisp border luminosity (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (`1`) shape profile**. Luxury heritage design demands structured, clean geometry reminiscent of cut gemstones, architectural archways, and traditional gold ingots.

- Base elements (buttons, inputs, cards) use an intentional `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, preserving architectural integrity while removing raw digital sharpness.
- Inner elements (pills, trust badges) scale to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`), ensuring certified seals and karat chips feel like minted insignias without becoming playful bubbles.
- Image containers occasionally incorporate an arch-top motif (straight bottom, gracefully rounded arch top) for bridal trousseau showcases, referencing royal Punjabi *jharokhas* (palace viewing balconies).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Heirloom Gold):** Solid antique gold fill (`#B8860B`) with deep charcoal text (`#12100E`), font weight 600, uppercase with `0.08em` tracking. Subtle gold specular gradient on hover.
- **Secondary Action (Palace Emerald):** Deep emerald base (`#1B4D3E`) with gold text (`#D4AF37`) for VIP Booking and Bespoke Bridal Consultations.
- **Tertiary / Ghost (Hairline Luxury):** Transparent background with `1px solid #B8860B` outline, transitioning to a soft parchment tint on hover.

### Product & Vitrine Cards
- Structured presentation featuring an off-white background (`#FDFBF7`) on light canvas or obsidian slate (`#211D1A`) on dark themes.
- Enclosed with a delicate `1px` antique gold border (`rgba(184, 134, 11, 0.2)`).
- Contains high-resolution jewelry photography, purity indicator flag (`22KT Yellow Gold`), hallmark seal, and real-time gold price linkage.

### BIS Hallmark & Trust Badges
- Precision micro-components displaying government-recognized hallmarks: BIS triangle logo, purity grade (`916` / `750`), assay center identification mark, and jeweller's identification.
- Styled with imperial emerald backgrounds (`rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)`), emerald border, and crisp monospaced weight breakdown.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat ivory fields framed by a subtle taupe-gold border (`#DDD5C7`). Focus state tightens to `1.5px solid #B8860B` with an ambient golden glow.
- Labels sit cleanly above the field in uppercase `label-md` tracking, accompanied by optional carat/weight unit suffixes.

### Gold Rate Ticker / Bar
- An authoritative, persistent top-level notification bar displaying today's 24KT and 22KT market rates per gram in Jagraon/Punjab. Set in high-contrast obsidian with metallic gold numerical highlights.

### Bespoke Bridal Consultation Drawer
- A sliding panel that mirrors an exclusive VIP salon room: rich dark backdrop, velvet-toned accents, and calendar slots for private viewing in the showroom.
